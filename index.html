Command flashcards

  • Sit: Lure up, mark the moment the rear touches the floor, reward.
  • Stay: Ask for stay, take one step, return and reward. Build distance slowly.
  • Come: Use an upbeat voice, reward generously when the dog reaches you.
  • Leave it: Reward the dog for disengaging from the item and looking back to you.
